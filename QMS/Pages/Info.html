When your visit to Emergency is completed, you will be given specific instructions and information, including the name of the physician or nurse practitioner who treated you. If you do not understand any of the instructions please speak with the nurse or physician that cared for you in the Emergency Department.


It is safe to ask questions. It is very important to ask questions if you do not understand any of the discharge information, or if you have any concerns about being discharged. Please ask any of the medical staff if you need any further information or have any questions before leaving the hospital.

Before you leave Emergency, make sure you understand:
  • Your Diagnosis.
  • Your Prescription.
  • The Treatment instructions and or advice.
  • Do you have any concerns about being discharged?
  • Is there anything else you might need to do at home?
